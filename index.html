Little Love Notes 💌

For my favourite human ♡

You don't even realise how much happiness you bring into my little world. Sometimes I look at you and wonder how I got so lucky to call you mine. 🥹

When the world feels heavy 🌙

My moon, you don't have to be strong all the time. You deserve softness, peace, and a hug that makes everything feel a little better. If I could, I'd hold you until every worry went quiet.

A little promise 🤍

I'll keep finding new ways to love you, new reasons to admire you, and new words to remind you that you mean more to me than I can explain.

Our Little Universe ✨

♡ My favourite person

Out of all the people in this world, somehow my heart found its favourite in you.

☾ My safe place

There is something about being with you that makes even ordinary moments feel special.

🌷 More memories to come

More silly conversations, more hugs, more pictures, more poems, and more little moments I will want to remember forever.

How to Share Our Book 🔗

Make a website link

1. Save this code as index.html.
2. Open GitHub Pages.
3. Follow its instructions to create a repository and upload your HTML file.
4. Enable Pages in the repository settings.
5. Open your published link and send it to him. ♡
